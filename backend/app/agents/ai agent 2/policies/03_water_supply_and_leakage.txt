
DOCUMENT_ID: DRAFT-WATER-001
TITLE: Municipal Water Supply and Leakage Handling
DOCUMENT_STATUS: DRAFT - NOT OFFICIAL MUNICIPAL POLICY
JURISDICTION: Generic municipal prototype
VERSION: 1.0

PURPOSE

This document provides provisional guidance for classifying and routing
water-supply complaints in the Samadhan AI prototype.

It does not establish official municipal responsibilities or deadlines.

SCOPE

This policy covers:
- Water pipeline leakage.
- Major water leakage in public areas.
- Interrupted water supply.
- Suspected water-quality concerns.
- Damage to water-supply infrastructure.

ISSUE IDENTIFICATION

Identify the reported water-related issue and preserve its original
issue ID.

Do not merge a water leak with a separate electrical, sanitation,
or infrastructure hazard simply because the issues share a location.

WATER PIPELINE LEAKAGE

When a leak is reported, evaluate:

- Reported leak size or intensity.
- Whether water is affecting a road, building, or public area.
- Whether access or essential services are disrupted.
- Whether infrastructure damage is reported.
- Whether a secondary hazard, such as an exposed electrical wire,
  is also reported.
- Whether the location and extent of the leak are known.

Do not invent the leak's size, duration, cause, or affected population.

PROVISIONAL SEVERITY GUIDANCE

LOW:
A limited leak with no reported significant disruption or identified
safety concern may be considered low for prototype testing.

MEDIUM:
A leak causing localized disruption or affecting a limited public area
may be considered medium when the available facts support that assessment.

HIGH:
A major leak causing substantial water loss, significant disruption,
road damage, or widespread impact may warrant a high-severity proposal.

CRITICAL:
Use only if the reported facts and applicable classification rules
support an immediate or extreme threat.

These are provisional examples, not official severity thresholds.
If facts are insufficient, use undetermined and request human review.

SECONDARY HAZARDS

A water leak may coincide with:
- An exposed electrical wire.
- Road damage or subsidence.
- Flooding that obstructs public access.
- Suspected contamination.

Assess each distinct issue independently.

Flag a potential immediate safety hazard for urgent human attention.
Do not infer that the water-related department owns the secondary hazard.

DEPARTMENT ROUTING

Candidate service category:
Water supply or water utility.

Before making an official assignment, verify the responsible authority
using municipality-specific jurisdiction records or official policy.

The responsible authority may differ depending on infrastructure
ownership and local administrative arrangements.

If jurisdiction cannot be established:
- Leave the department assignment unresolved.
- Explain the evidence gap.
- Set requires_human_review to true.

WATER SUPPLY INTERRUPTION

For a reported interruption, identify:
- The affected location, if known.
- Whether the interruption is localized or widespread.
- The reported duration, if provided.
- Any official service-restoration guidance found in retrieved evidence.

Do not invent a restoration estimate or claim that an official outage
has been confirmed.

WATER QUALITY CONCERNS

For suspected contamination:
- Record the reported concern accurately.
- Flag the need for appropriate human assessment.
- Retrieve applicable official water-quality and public-health guidance.
- Do not diagnose contamination based solely on an unverified complaint.
- Do not invent laboratory results or safety declarations.

SLA AND COMPLETION TIMELINES

This document defines no official response deadline, completion time,
or service-level agreement.

Only provide an official timeline when a verified, applicable policy
supports it.

If no applicable SLA evidence is available, set completion_timeline
to null and request human review where appropriate.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Distinguish:
- Verified official policy evidence.
- Project-authored prototype guidance.
- Unverified complaint information.

Do not present this draft as an official municipal regulation.

END OF DOCUMENT
