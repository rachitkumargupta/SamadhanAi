
DOCUMENT_ID: DRAFT-ROADS-DRAINAGE-001
TITLE: Municipal Roads, Potholes and Drainage Complaints
DOCUMENT_STATUS: DRAFT - NOT OFFICIAL MUNICIPAL POLICY
JURISDICTION: Generic municipal prototype
VERSION: 1.0

PURPOSE

Provide provisional guidance for classifying and routing complaints
related to roads, public infrastructure, drainage and waterlogging.

This document is a project-authored draft and does not establish
official municipal responsibilities or response deadlines.

SCOPE

- Potholes and damaged road surfaces.
- Damaged public footpaths.
- Blocked stormwater drains.
- Localized waterlogging.
- Damaged drainage infrastructure.
- Road obstructions caused by infrastructure damage.

ISSUE IDENTIFICATION

Identify each distinct issue and preserve its original issue ID.

Determine whether the complaint concerns road maintenance, stormwater
drainage, sewerage, or another infrastructure service.

Do not assume that all drains are managed by the same authority.

PROVISIONAL SEVERITY GUIDANCE

LOW:
A minor infrastructure defect with limited reported impact and no
identified immediate safety concern.

MEDIUM:
A localized pothole, blocked drain, or similar issue causing noticeable
inconvenience or limited disruption.

HIGH:
Substantial road damage, extensive waterlogging, significant access
disruption, or an infrastructure defect presenting a serious hazard.

CRITICAL:
Use only when the available facts support an immediate or extreme
threat to people or another severe consequence under applicable rules.

These classifications are provisional examples, not official municipal
severity thresholds. If facts are insufficient, use undetermined and
request human review.

SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS

Consider whether the reported issue creates:
- A risk to pedestrians or road users.
- Access problems for emergency services.
- Significant flooding or waterlogging.
- Possible infrastructure collapse or subsidence.
- A secondary hazard, such as an exposed electrical wire.

Flag potential immediate hazards for urgent human attention.

Do not invent measurements, damage assessments, traffic conditions,
or facts that are not present in the complaint or supporting evidence.

DEPARTMENT AND JURISDICTION ROUTING

Candidate service categories:
- Road maintenance.
- Stormwater drainage.
- Sewerage.
- Public infrastructure maintenance.

These are candidate categories, not confirmed department assignments.

Verify responsibility using municipality-specific official records.
Road ownership and maintenance may depend on the road type and
the relevant public authority.

If jurisdiction is uncertain:
- Leave the department assignment unresolved.
- Explain the missing information.
- Set requires_human_review to true.

DRAINAGE AND WATERLOGGING

For drainage complaints, assess:
- Whether a blockage or overflow is reported.
- The reported extent of waterlogging.
- Whether access or public services are disrupted.
- Whether a secondary safety hazard is reported.
- Whether the issue concerns stormwater or sewage, if known.

Do not infer contamination or a particular drainage-system failure
without supporting information.

MULTI-ISSUE COMPLAINTS

If a damaged road and an exposed electrical wire are reported together,
assess each issue independently.

Create or recommend separate child tasks where appropriate.
Preserve all supplied issue IDs and flag any potential immediate hazard.

SLA AND COMPLETION TIMELINES

This draft defines no official response deadlines, repair timelines,
or escalation procedures.

Provide an official completion timeline only when verified, applicable
policy evidence supports it.

If no applicable SLA is available, set completion_timeline to null.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Distinguish verified official policy from project-authored draft guidance
and unverified complaint information.

Do not present this document as an official municipal regulation.

END OF DOCUMENT
