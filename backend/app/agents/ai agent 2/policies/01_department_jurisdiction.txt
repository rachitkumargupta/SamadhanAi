TITLE: Generic Municipal Department Jurisdiction and Routing
DOCUMENT_STATUS: DRAFT - NOT OFFICIAL MUNICIPAL POLICY
JURISDICTION: Generic municipal prototype
VERSION: 1.0

PURPOSE
This document defines provisional routing guidance for the Samadhan AI
Resolution and Routing Agent. It supports prototype testing only.
It does not establish legally binding municipal responsibilities.

SOURCE STATUS
This is a project-authored draft. Department names and mappings must be
verified against the relevant municipality's official organization,
service charter, grievance portal, or published regulations before
production use.

GENERAL ROUTING RULES

1. Identify each distinct issue in the complaint.
2. Recommend a department only when retrieved evidence supports the
   recommendation.
3. Treat the mappings in this draft as candidate categories, not verified
   assignments to a real municipal department.
4. If the responsible authority is unclear, return an unresolved department
   recommendation and request human review.
5. If an issue may involve multiple authorities, propose separate child
   tasks where appropriate and flag jurisdiction review.
6. Do not invent department names, officer names, ward responsibilities,
   escalation contacts, or official service deadlines.
7. A municipality may delegate a service to a state agency, utility,
   contractor, or another public authority. Verify the local arrangement.

PROVISIONAL SERVICE CATEGORIES

CATEGORY: WATER SUPPLY AND PIPELINE LEAKAGE
Candidate responsibility:
Water supply or water-utility function.

Example issues:
- Water pipeline leakage
- Interrupted water supply
- Suspected water-quality problem

Routing condition:
Use this category to retrieve relevant policy evidence. Confirm the actual
responsible authority before making an official department recommendation.

CATEGORY: SEWERAGE AND DRAINAGE
Candidate responsibility:
Sewerage, drainage, or stormwater-management function.

Example issues:
- Blocked drain
- Sewer overflow
- Waterlogging
- Damaged stormwater drain

Routing condition:
Distinguish sewerage from stormwater drainage when the evidence permits.
If the issue type or responsible authority is unclear, request human review.

CATEGORY: SOLID WASTE AND SANITATION
Candidate responsibility:
Municipal sanitation or solid-waste-management function.

Example issues:
- Uncollected garbage
- Public-area waste accumulation
- Street-cleaning concern

Routing condition:
Verify the municipality's actual service ownership and complaint categories.

CATEGORY: ROADS AND PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE
Candidate responsibility:
Road maintenance or public-infrastructure function.

Example issues:
- Potholes
- Damaged public road
- Damaged roadside infrastructure

Routing condition:
Determine whether the road is maintained by the municipality or another
authority. If ownership is unknown, request human review.

CATEGORY: STREET LIGHTING AND ELECTRICAL HAZARDS
Candidate responsibility:
Street-lighting or electrical-maintenance function, subject to local
jurisdiction and infrastructure ownership.

Example issues:
- Non-functional street light
- Exposed electrical wire
- Damaged electrical equipment in a public area

Routing condition:
An exposed wire or suspected live electrical hazard must be flagged for
urgent human attention. This flag is a safety recommendation, not an
official municipal SLA or a confirmed department assignment.
Do not recommend that the public touch or attempt to repair the hazard.

MULTI-ISSUE COMPLAINTS

If a complaint reports a major water leak and an exposed electrical wire:

1. Preserve both issue IDs from the input.
2. Propose separate child tasks when the issues require different handling.
3. Retrieve evidence relevant to each issue.
4. Assess the responsible authority for each issue independently.
5. Flag the potential electrical hazard for urgent human attention.
6. Do not assume that both issues belong to the same department.
7. If official jurisdiction cannot be established, leave the assignment
   unresolved and require human review.

HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED WHEN

- No relevant policy evidence is retrieved.
- Evidence does not establish the responsible authority.
- The municipality or ward jurisdiction is unknown.
- Multiple authorities may be responsible.
- The issue falls outside the available policy documents.
- The proposed assignment conflicts with retrieved evidence.

AUTHORITY AND SLA LIMITATIONS

This draft does not define official severity levels, statutory duties,
completion deadlines, service-level agreements, or escalation procedures.

These details must be obtained from applicable, verified official sources.
Do not treat candidate service categories as proof of jurisdiction.
