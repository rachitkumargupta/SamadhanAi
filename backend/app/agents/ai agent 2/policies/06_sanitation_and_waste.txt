
DOCUMENT_ID: DRAFT-SANITATION-001
TITLE: Municipal Sanitation and Solid Waste Complaints
DOCUMENT_STATUS: DRAFT - NOT OFFICIAL MUNICIPAL POLICY
JURISDICTION: Generic municipal prototype
VERSION: 1.0

PURPOSE

Provide provisional guidance for classifying and routing complaints
related to municipal sanitation and solid waste.

This document is a project-authored draft. It does not establish
official department responsibilities or response deadlines.

SCOPE

- Uncollected household or public-area waste.
- Garbage accumulation.
- Missed waste collection.
- Public-area cleanliness concerns.
- Overflowing waste bins.
- Reported sanitation-related hazards.

ISSUE IDENTIFICATION

Identify the specific sanitation issue and preserve its original issue ID.

Distinguish between routine waste collection, public-area cleaning,
drainage blockage, sewage overflow, and other reported problems.

Do not assume that all sanitation services are managed by one department.

PROVISIONAL SEVERITY GUIDANCE

LOW:
A localized cleanliness concern with limited reported impact and no
identified immediate safety concern.

MEDIUM:
Accumulated waste or missed collection causing noticeable inconvenience
or affecting a public area.

HIGH:
Extensive waste accumulation, significant disruption, or a reported
condition that may create a serious public-health or environmental concern.

CRITICAL:
Use only when available evidence supports an immediate or extreme threat
under the applicable classification rules.

These are provisional examples, not official municipal thresholds.
If the available facts do not support classification, use undetermined
and request human review.

PUBLIC-HEALTH CONSIDERATIONS

Consider the reported extent, location, duration, and potential exposure.

Do not diagnose an illness, confirm contamination, or assert the presence
of hazardous material without supporting evidence.

If the complaint reports a potential immediate hazard, flag it for
urgent human attention and explain the reported concern.

DEPARTMENT AND JURISDICTION ROUTING

Candidate service categories:
- Solid waste management.
- Sanitation.
- Public-area cleaning.

These are candidate categories, not verified assignments to a particular
municipality or public authority.

Verify the responsible authority through official municipal records.

If jurisdiction is uncertain:
- Leave the department assignment unresolved.
- Explain the missing information.
- Set requires_human_review to true.

MULTI-ISSUE COMPLAINTS

If a complaint reports accumulated garbage alongside a blocked drain,
assess the sanitation and drainage issues independently.

Create or recommend separate child tasks when appropriate.
Preserve every supplied issue ID.

SLA AND COMPLETION TIMELINES

This draft defines no official collection schedule, cleanup deadline,
completion timeline, or escalation procedure.

Provide an official deadline only when supported by verified,
applicable policy evidence.

If no applicable SLA is available, set completion_timeline to null.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Distinguish verified official policy from project-authored draft guidance
and unverified complaint information.

Do not present this document as an official municipal regulation.

END OF DOCUMENT
