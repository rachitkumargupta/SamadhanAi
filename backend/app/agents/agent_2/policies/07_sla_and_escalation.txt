
DOCUMENT_ID: DRAFT-SLA-001
TITLE: Municipal Service Levels and Escalation Rules
DOCUMENT_STATUS: DRAFT - NOT OFFICIAL MUNICIPAL POLICY
JURISDICTION: Generic municipal prototype
VERSION: 1.0

PURPOSE

Define how Samadhan AI Agent 2 handles service-level agreements (SLAs),
completion timelines, and escalation recommendations during prototype
testing.

This document does not establish official response deadlines,
statutory obligations, or municipal escalation procedures.

DEFINITIONS

ACKNOWLEDGEMENT TIME:
The time allowed by an applicable policy to acknowledge a complaint.

RESPONSE TIME:
The time allowed by an applicable policy to begin responding to a complaint.

RESOLUTION TIME:
The time allowed by an applicable policy to resolve a complaint.

ESCALATION:
A recommendation to refer a complaint to an appropriate higher authority
or designated process when applicable rules or conditions require it.

These time categories are distinct. Do not treat them as interchangeable.

SLA EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Before recommending an official SLA, identify:

- The applicable policy or service charter.
- The issuing authority.
- The relevant service or complaint category.
- The applicable deadline or time limit.
- The conditions and exclusions, if specified.
- The source document and supporting excerpt.

Only use a deadline when the retrieved evidence supports its applicability
to the specific complaint and jurisdiction.

Do not invent a deadline, convert a severity level into an assumed
deadline, or copy a deadline from an unrelated service category.

MISSING OR INSUFFICIENT SLA EVIDENCE

If no applicable official SLA is retrieved:

- Set completion_timeline to null.
- Explain that an applicable official deadline was not established.
- Request human review when necessary.
- Do not represent a prototype suggestion as an official deadline.

An empty policy folder does not establish that a municipality has no SLA.
It means the system currently lacks retrieved evidence.

PRIORITY AND SEVERITY

Severity and SLA are related but distinct.

Severity describes the assessed seriousness of an issue.
An SLA defines a time requirement under an applicable policy.

Do not automatically assign a deadline based only on a severity label.

If the complaint reports a potential immediate hazard, flag it for urgent
human attention even when the official SLA is unavailable.

This safety flag does not create an SLA or start a service-level clock.

ESCALATION RECOMMENDATIONS

Recommend escalation only when a verified policy or documented procedure
supports the recommendation.

When applicable escalation rules are unavailable:

- Do not invent escalation levels or escalation contacts.
- Do not claim that an escalation has occurred.
- Do not assign an officer or send a notification.
- Explain that the escalation procedure requires verification.

HUMAN REVIEW CONDITIONS

Human review is required or recommended when:

- The relevant SLA cannot be established.
- Retrieved documents conflict.
- The complaint category does not match the policy.
- The jurisdiction or responsible authority is uncertain.
- The deadline's applicability is unclear.
- A potential immediate hazard requires assessment by the appropriate
  authority.

AGENT 2 RESPONSIBILITIES

Agent 2 may:
- Retrieve relevant SLA documents.
- Extract applicable time requirements.
- Explain the evidence supporting an SLA recommendation.
- Identify missing information.
- Recommend human review.
- Propose a policy-supported escalation.

Agent 2 must not:
- Create a ticket.
- Assign an officer.
- Start or stop an SLA clock.
- Send an escalation or emergency notification.
- Claim that an operational action has occurred.

Those actions belong to the appropriate downstream system or authorized
human process.

AUDITABILITY

Every official SLA recommendation should retain:
- Policy identifier.
- Source document.
- Supporting excerpt.
- Applicable complaint category.
- Recommended deadline.
- Any conditions affecting applicability.

If the source cannot be verified, do not label the deadline official.

DOCUMENT STATUS

This document is project-authored draft guidance for prototype testing.
It is not an official municipal service-level agreement.

END OF DOCUMENT
